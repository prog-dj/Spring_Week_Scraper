Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

Springr ("we", "the service") is a spring-week and insight-programme application tracker. This page explains what personal data we collect, why, and what rights you have over it, in line with the UK GDPR / EU GDPR.

What we collect

  • Account data — when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile picture, and a unique Google account identifier. We never see or store your Google password.
  • Data you provide — opportunities you save, applications you track, notes you write in an application workspace, and any documents you upload (e.g. CV, cover letter, transcript).
  • Technical data — your IP address is used transiently to rate-limit login and upload attempts (abuse prevention); these counters expire automatically within about an hour and are not linked to your account or retained long-term.
  • Error monitoring — if enabled, technical error reports (e.g. a crashed request) may be sent to our error-tracking provider. These are designed to capture technical context, not the contents of your documents.

Why we collect it

We process your data to provide the service you've signed up for (performance of a contract) — storing your saved opportunities, applications, and documents so they're available whenever you sign back in — and, for IP-based rate limiting, to prevent abuse of the service (legitimate interest).

Cookies

We use only strictly necessary cookies:

  • springr_session — keeps you signed in. Expires after 30 days or when you sign out.
  • springr_oauth_state — a short-lived cookie (10 minutes) used only during the Google sign-in process, to prevent cross-site request forgery.

We do not use advertising, tracking, or analytics cookies. Because these cookies are strictly necessary for the service to function, they don't require separate consent under PECR/GDPR — but we're telling you about them anyway.

Where your data is stored

Your account and application data is stored in Cloudflare D1 (database) and uploaded documents in Cloudflare R2 (file storage), both part of Cloudflare's infrastructure.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for marketing. Google is used only to authenticate your sign-in, under Google's own privacy policy. If error monitoring is enabled, our error-tracking provider processes technical crash reports.

How long we keep your data

Your account data is kept until you delete your account (see below). Rate-limit counters expire automatically within about an hour.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access & portability — download everything stored on your account as a JSON file, from the Documents page ("Download my data").
  • Erasure — permanently delete your account and everything tied to it, from the Documents page ("Delete my account"). This is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Rectification — your name/email/photo come from Google; update them at the source (your Google account) and they'll sync on your next sign-in.
  • Objection — you can stop using the service and delete your account at any time.

Children

This service is intended for people old enough to be applying for spring-week/insight programmes (typically 17+) and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the "last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact
